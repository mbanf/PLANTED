\abstract{
\textbf{Motivation:} Predicting gene expression across tissues and developmental stages is central to understanding gene regulation in crops. Existing approaches such as DeepWheat train separate convolutional models per tissue, limiting cross-tissue knowledge sharing and scalability. Plant genomic foundation models offer rich, pretrained sequence representations but have not yet been applied to multi-tissue expression prediction in a unified framework.\\
\textbf{Results:} We present \mytool, a lightweight multi-tissue gene expression predictor for wheat (\textit{Triticum aestivum}) that combines LoRA-adapted embeddings from the 318M-parameter \botanic BiMamba2 foundation model with epigenomic features and tissue-specific FiLM conditioning. Using a single model with only 1.0M trainable head parameters, \mytool achieves a Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC) of 0.910 on log-transformed expression and 0.816 on TPM-scale expression across six tissues, substantially outperforming DeepWheat (mean PCC\,=\,0.461 across six tissues, with negative $R^2$ on developmental tissues) which requires separate tissue-specific models totalling 245M parameters. LoRA adaptation of the foundation model backbone improves PCC from 0.744 (frozen) to 0.910, demonstrating that task-specific fine-tuning of genomic foundation models is essential for expression prediction. We further show through ablation that sequence embeddings contribute significantly beyond epigenomic features alone ($\Delta$PCC\,=\,+0.063).\\
\textbf{Availability:} \mytool is available at \url{https://github.com/mbanf/PLANTED}.\\
